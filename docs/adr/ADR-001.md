# ADR-001: Modular Monolith Architecture

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

PulseFlow requires a clear separation of responsibilities without introducing the operational complexity of microservices during initial development.

## Decision

Use a modular monolith architecture with clearly separated application domains and independently deployable applications.

The architecture will maintain strong module boundaries and avoid unnecessary cross-module dependencies.

## Consequences

- Easier initial development and debugging.
- Clear domain ownership.
- Lower infrastructure complexity than microservices.
- Requires discipline to prevent tightly coupled modules.
- Individual applications can be extracted or scaled independently where necessary.
